# Personal DX windows

[Deutsch](DX-Zeitfenster) | [English](Personal-DX-windows)

In **Who hears me?**, **Personal DX windows** opens a local reception history. It helps identify repeatedly observed paths by DXCC country, band, UTC hour and month.

## Collect observations

1. Use the callsign and mode actually transmitted.
2. Leave the PSK Reporter window open; regular queries extend the local history.
3. Enter a **Station / antenna setup** name.
4. After changing your antenna, location or power, use a new name and leave the field to apply it.

Callsign, mode and station/antenna setup remain separate. There are no historical back-queries. Repeated queries count each receiver only once per UTC date and hour.

## Explore the history

| Selection | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Most observed windows | Ordered by distinct reception days, then receiver-days. |
| Next 24 hours (UTC) | Next recurrence of previously observed hourly windows. |
| Month / country or band | Narrow the observations. |
| Minimum reception days | Hide windows observed on too few distinct days. |
| All / Last 90 / Last 365 UTC days | Entire history or a limited period. |

The table shows reception days, receivers, receiver-days, and first/last observations. Months may combine observations from different calendar years.

**This is not a live propagation forecast or a QSO probability.** “Now” means that the historical UTC hour is currently in progress. Missing reports do not prove a closed radio path: activity, power and reporting coverage affect the observations.
